Privacy

Your privacy

What we keep, what we do not, and who can see it.

What we keep

  • Your codename and a scrambled (hashed) form of your password. We cannot read your password.
  • Your scores, your best night and, if you press Save in the game, your saved game with a small photo of the screen.
  • The time you joined and signed in, and whether you played on a phone or a computer.
  • Our server writes your IP address in its log to protect against abuse.

What we do not ask for

  • No e-mail address, no phone number, no real name, no location.

Cookies and storage

  • One cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. It is not used for advertising.
  • Your browser keeps the game files and your language choice on your device so the next visit starts at once. You can remove them with the Remove files button.

reCAPTCHA

When you create an agent, Google reCAPTCHA checks that you are a person. Google's own privacy policy and terms apply to that check.

Who can see it

Your codename, score and rank are shown on the public high score board. Nothing else about you is public. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers.

Your choices

To delete your agent and all its data, or to ask a question, contact the team. See the license section.
