Your shift

Detect. Catch. Seal. Upgrade.

Every night in the house follows the same work. Learn it once and the dark gets easier.

01

Detect

Take out the EMF scanner and switch it on. Its lights climb as a ghost gets closer, and the red light means she is right here.

02

Catch

The Phantom Gun pulls an orb out of the dark. Hold the pull, keep it aimed, and watch the heat: a hot gun lets the orb run.

03

Seal

Walk the orb to the generator. It makes a MEP capsule. Put the MEP in a Faraday cage and hand it in at the Drop Table.

04

Upgrade

The NPC checks your cage and gives you a point. Spend it on the gun, the EM unit, the MEP or the radio.

8 PMThe ghosts appear. The orbs hide in the rooms.
10 PMThe woman starts to walk the house. Hide when she comes.
6 AMDawn. You survived the night. The scanner battery is charged.
7 AM to 4 PMThe NPC is at the table. Hand in cages and upgrade.
